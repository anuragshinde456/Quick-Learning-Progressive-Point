About Quick Progressive Career Point

Quick Progressive Career Point is Odisha's premier 1-on-1 personalized home tutoring platform, connecting ambitious students with verified expert home tutors across Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, Rourkela, Sambalpur, Berhampur, Balasore & statewide.

Our Mission

To provide top quality, personalized 1-on-1 home tutoring directly at students' doorsteps across Odisha. We conduct thorough in-person and video evaluations of every tutor before publishing their profile to ensure safety, subject mastery, and empathetic mentoring.

Our Vision

To build Odisha's most trusted home education network where parents and students can transparently evaluate teachers through 30s-5min intro video lectures, verified qualifications, and direct communication, all while preserving student contact privacy.
